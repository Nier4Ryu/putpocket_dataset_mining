## Exact views and formulas

The hop-only and equal-hop cumulative position heatmaps preserve absolute
token identity/order and shade frozen Q1 `[117,2069)` and Q2 `[2088,2103)`.
Both use the same fixed display transform and scale:

`C_L[i] = log10(max(p_L[i], 1e-12))`, limits `[-12,0]`.

The display floor changes no stored probability. Separate sorted figures rank
all N=2,103 probabilities descending, break ties by lower absolute position,
and retain every rank plus the complete cumulative mass. Sorted curves compare
shape/concentration and intentionally do not preserve identity.

For identity-aligned normalized P and Q over all token positions,

`JSD_2(P,Q)=0.5*KL_2(P||M)+0.5*KL_2(Q||M)`, `M=(P+Q)/2`,

and the matrix reports `sqrt(JSD_2)` in `[0,1]`. Every matrix is symmetric with
an exact-zero diagonal. The summary also retains entropy, effective support,
nonzero support, maximum/minimum-positive probability, Herfindahl
concentration, and full-vector Gini at every level.
